 STAR Method Guide

              

                
S
Situation
Set the context — describe the challenge or scenario you faced.

                
T
Task
What was your specific responsibility or goal in that situation?

                
A
Action
Describe the specific steps you took to address the situation.

                
R
Result
What was the outcome? Use numbers and metrics where possible.

              

              

                
                Pro Tip: Structure every behavioral answer using STAR. Aim for 2-3 minute responses with specific examples and quantifiable results.

 HR Interview Preparation

          
Master HR interviews with proven strategies, sample answers, and expert guidance

        

        

          
          

            
 HR Preparation Roadmap

            

              
Research the Company
Understand their products, culture, recent news, and mission statement.

              
Prepare Your "Tell Me About Yourself"
Craft a 90-second professional story: background → skills → goals.

              
Practice Behavioral Questions
Prepare 10 STAR-method stories covering different competencies.

              
Prepare Smart Questions to Ask
Always have 3-5 thoughtful questions ready for the interviewer.

              
Negotiate Salary Confidently
Research market rates and practice your counter-offer response.

            

          


          
          

            
 Salary Negotiation Tips

            

              
01
Know Your Market Value
Research glassdoor, AmbitionBox & LinkedIn Salary for your role + city + experience.

              
02
Never Give First Number
Defer with: "I'd love to understand the full package before discussing a number."

              
03
Anchor High (But Realistic)
Ask for 20-30% above your minimum acceptable. Always negotiate variable + ESOPs.

              
04
Justify With Value
Say: "Based on my X years delivering Y results, I'm targeting Z LPA."

              
05
Silence is Power
After stating your number, stop talking. Let them respond first.

            

          


          
          

            
 Strengths & Weaknesses Framework

            

              

                
✅ Presenting Strengths

                
Choose strengths directly relevant to the role — not generic ones like "hardworking".

                
Back each strength with a brief specific example: "I'm detail-oriented — in my last role I caught a critical API bug that prevented a $50K loss."

                
Limit to 2-3 strengths max. Quality over quantity.

              

              

                
⚠️ Presenting Weaknesses

                
Choose a real weakness that won't disqualify you — not a strength in disguise.

                
Always follow with what you're doing to improve: "I used to struggle with delegation, so I've been using Notion to assign clearer task ownership."

                
Never say: "I work too hard" or "I'm a perfectionist." Interviewers see through this.

              

            

          


          
          

            
 Corporate Etiquette Guide

            

              
Punctuality
Join online interviews 5 min early. In-person, arrive 10-15 min before.

              
Dress Code
Business casual minimum. When unsure, over-dress — it signals professionalism.

              
Eye Contact
Maintain steady eye contact (not staring). Look at camera in video interviews.

              
Phone Etiquette
Silence all devices. Never check phone during an interview — ever.

              
Follow-Up Email
Send a thank-you email within 24 hours referencing a specific conversation point.

              
Active Listening
Nod, take brief notes, and never interrupt. Pause before answering complex questions.

 Resume Tips

            
    
              
  • Use action verbs to start every bullet point: Engineered, Designed, Scaled, Led.
    • 
              
  • Quantify achievements: "Reduced load time by 40%" beats "improved performance".
    • 
              
  • Keep it to 1 page for under 5 years of experience — 2 pages max for senior roles.
    • 
              
  • Use a simple, ATS-safe font: Calibri, Arial, or Helvetica. No fancy fonts.
    • 
              
  • Include a LinkedIn URL and GitHub link — recruiters always check.
    • 
            

          


          
          

            
 Interview Tips

            
    
              
  • Research the company's latest product launches and news — mention them in the interview.
    • 
              
  • Prepare 3 stories for: a challenge you overcame, a team conflict, and your greatest achievement.
    • 
              
  • Silence after a tough question is okay — say "let me think for a moment."
    • 
              
  • Mirror the interviewer's communication style — formal or casual.
    • 
              
  • Always send a thank-you email within 24 hours — only 10% of candidates do this.
    • 
            

          


          
          

            
 Productivity Tips

            
    
              
  • Apply to jobs in the morning between 8-10 AM — applications get more visibility.
    • 
              
  • Use the Pomodoro technique: 25 min deep work, 5 min break for job searching.
    • 
              
  • Set a weekly target: apply to at least 5-10 jobs, do 2 mock interviews.
    • 
              
  • Track all applications in a spreadsheet — follow up after 7 days.
    • 
              
  • Join LinkedIn communities in your niche — 60% of jobs are filled via referrals.
